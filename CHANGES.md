# What changed in this version

**Reframed:** from "the wrong version of you" to "see and challenge the reasoning before your agent acts".

- **Tags:** the four failure modes (Human context, Human growth, Decision flexibility, Commercial influence) replace Past / Vulnerable / Agreeable you.
- **Council:** six defined roles (Present Self, Future Self, Values & Commitments, The Skeptic, The Optimizer, Influence Auditor) replace The Friend and The Empty Chair. It is convened on demand and ends in a decision brief. The room folds away once heard.
- **Living Memory:** split into Intentions (with experiment or lasting, hard limit or flexible target, last confirmed), Context you added, Past and inferred, Signal permissions, and Your data.
- **Gate:** factors are now stakes, reversibility, uncertainty, conflict with your intentions, and emotional heat. Hard limits are never relaxed silently.
- **Influence:** each claim is labelled Verified sponsored, Suspected, Unknown status or No sponsorship found, with its evidence.
- **Clarifying questions:** Sabha asks "hard limit or flexible target?" and "what has changed that the AI agent can't see?" inside the case.
- **North star:** Informed Agency Rate, checked live on every decision, with supporting metrics on Insights.
- **Scenarios:** the 5 am routine and the ₹60,000 budget were added. The gym renewal and job counter-offer were removed to keep the focus on the four acceptance tests.
- **Rules:** a short "what Sabha can and can't see" note sets the boundary.
