## Short description (≤260 chars)

Sabha sits on top of your AI agent. It surfaces assumptions, trade-offs and commercial influence, runs a six-role Council when stakes are high, and lets you decide what gets acted on — measured by Informed Agency Rate.

## First comment (maker)

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I’m launching **Sabha** — a layer that makes the reasoning behind your AI agent’s decisions visible before anything irreversible happens.

**The problem:** agents act on an incomplete picture of you. They miss reconciliations, treat old habits as destiny, take budgets too literally, and hide sponsorship or emotional targeting.

**What Sabha does:**
1. **Living Memory** — intentions and context you add outrank stale history  
2. **Council** — six roles challenge the proposal; ends in a brief, not a vote  
3. **Gate** — routes by stakes / reversibility / uncertainty / conflict / heat  

**Try the interactive demo** (simulated agent, real product logic): [your-url]/demo

We’re opening early access starting with purchase decisions. Would love feedback on whether the Informed Agency Rate feels like the right north star.
