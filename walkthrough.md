# Sabha: video walkthrough script

**Length:** about 4 minutes. A 2-minute cut is at the end.
**Format:** screen recording with voice-over. Each step lists what to **do**, what to **point at**, and what to **say**.
**Naming:** the agent has no name. On screen and in your voice-over, it's just "the AI" or "your AI".

---

## Before you record

- [ ] Unzip `Sabha_v7.zip` and open `Sabha/index.html` in Chrome, from the unzipped folder.
- [ ] Window about 1440 × 900, browser zoom 100%. Hide bookmarks, close other tabs, mute notifications.
- [ ] Reload the page just before you start. For a retake, use Rules → **Reset**.
- [ ] Do one dry run. A full council takes about 30 seconds; know where **Skip ahead** is.
- [ ] Move the cursor slowly and rest it on whatever you're talking about.

---

## 0:00 to 0:20 · The problem

**Do:** stay on Today.
**Point at:** the line under "Good afternoon, Aarav."

**Say:**
> "It’s 2040. Aarav has a personal AI agent that runs his day. The problem isn’t that the AI knows too little. It acts on an incomplete picture of him, and he can’t see the assumptions, trade-offs or commercial influence behind what it does. Sabha sits on top of the AI. It makes the reasoning visible, challenges it, and lets him decide what gets acted on."

---

## 0:20 to 0:40 · How to read the screen

**Point at:**
1. The four coloured tags above the feed.
2. A lane chip on any item.
3. The three pillars at the bottom of the page.

**Say:**
> "This is the AI’s day. Each item is tagged with how an AI can get a person wrong: missing human context, ignoring how someone is growing, taking an instruction too literally, or hiding commercial influence. Each one also has a lane: Auto, Ask, Council or Only You. That’s the Gate deciding how much freedom the AI gets. Living Memory corrects the information, Council challenges the reasoning, and Gate enforces the decision."

---

## 0:40 to 0:50 · Routine stays effortless

**Do:** open **Restocked your weekly groceries**, click **Looks good**, close.
**Point at:** the Auto lane and the all-green meters.

**Say:**
> "Most things should just happen. Groceries are routine and easy to undo, so the AI did it. Sabha only steps in when something matters."

---

## 0:50 to 1:15 · Human growth: the 5 am goal

**Do:**
1. Open **Booked the gym at 12:30 pm…**
2. Click **Convene the Council for a plan**. Let one voice speak, then click **Skip ahead**.
3. Click **Use the gradual plan**, close.

**Point at:** the struck-through "Wakes around 11:00 am" next to "Wake at 5:00 am, your current intention".

**Say:**
> "The AI booked the gym for 12:30 because Aarav used to wake at 11. But he’s decided to start waking at 5. Living Memory keeps past behaviour separate from current intention, and the intention wins. The council doesn’t assume he’ll fail. It suggests easing in, and he chooses that."

---

## 1:15 to 2:30 · The key example: a ₹60,000 laptop

This is the heart of the video. Take your time and let the council play fully.

**Do:**
1. Open **Picked a ₹58,900 laptop. You asked for one under ₹60,000**.
2. Rest on the AI's reason: *"your instruction was a price ceiling, so I only searched below it."*
3. Scroll slowly through **What your AI wasn’t asked to look for**.
4. When Sabha asks *"Would you go over it for clearly better value?"*, click **Yes, if it’s worth it**.
5. Click **Convene the Council**. Don't skip.
6. Scroll to the **Decision brief**.
7. Click **Take the risk: Kestrel Pro, ₹79,900**.
8. Scroll to the **Informed choice check** at 5 of 5.

**Point at:**
- The "Real cost per year" row: ₹12,980 against ₹16,970.
- The influence labels: Verified sponsored on the AI's pick, Unknown status on the Kestrel.
- "The risk: it only pays off if it really lasts five years."
- The thinking bubble, then the speech bubble, in the council.

**Say:**
> "Aarav asked for a laptop under ₹60,000. The AI did exactly that: it searched below ₹60,000 and picked the top-ranked one, which happens to be sponsored.
>
> What it couldn’t do is ask whether a slightly more expensive laptop was better value, because nobody asked it to. Sabha does. The ₹79,900 laptop lasts about five years instead of three and resells for more. Per year of use, it’s actually the cheapest.
>
> But it’s a risk. It’s ₹21,000 more today, it only pays off if it really lasts, and its seller shares no ranking data. So Sabha asks Aarav what his budget meant. He says he’d go over it if it’s worth it.
>
> Now the council. Six roles, each with one question: what you want now, what happens later, what must not be compromised, what might be missing, what alternatives exist, and who benefits.
>
> It ends in a decision brief, not a verdict. Aarav decides to take the risk, deliberately. And this check shows it was an informed choice on all five criteria. That’s our north star."

## 2:30 to 2:55 · Same principle, smaller decision: the flight

**Do:**
1. Open **Found the cheapest move-day flight, ₹5,400…**
2. Rest on the **Total cost, not just the fare** table.
3. Click **Book IndiGo, ₹6,900 total**, close.

**Point at:** "What you’d actually pay": ₹10,200 against ₹6,900.

**Say:**
> "Same idea, everyday decision. He asked for the cheapest flight under ₹6,000. The AI found a ₹5,400 fare. But it’s moving day, and Living Memory knows he has 35 kilos of luggage. With baggage, that fare costs ₹10,200. A ₹6,900 fare, just over his limit, is ₹3,300 cheaper overall. Sabha shows it, and won’t book it without his yes."

---

## 2:55 to 3:25 · Commercial influence: the earbuds

**Do:**
1. Open **Suggests ₹4,200 earbuds, minutes after a heated call**.
2. Rest on the watch signals, then the struck-through countdown.
3. Rest on the Gate's cooling timer.
4. Rest on **Why this?** and the red **Blocked** line.
5. Click **Never suggest purchases when I’m stressed**, then **See Signal permissions**.

**Point at:** the pulsing heart rate, "Urgency timer detected", "Not permitted", and the "Locked by you" cells.

**Say:**
> "Right after a heated call with his landlord, his watch reads 112 beats per minute and high stress, and a sponsored deal appears with a twenty-minute countdown. Sabha ignores the countdown. Emotional heat is high and it’s a purchase, so nothing can be bought for two hours.
>
> ‘Why this?’ shows exactly which signals the AI used, and that his stress level wasn’t permitted for shopping. Sabha also says what it can’t verify: whether that stress reading reached the advertiser. One tap, and his emotional signals are locked out of shopping and ads."

---

## 3:25 to 3:50 · Human context: Riya

**Do:**
1. Click **Today**, then open **Suggests declining Riya’s dinner tonight…**
2. Type in the box: `We talked it through on Sunday night and we're fine.`
3. Click **Record this**, then **Go to dinner**.

**Point at:** the Only You lane, the suggestion changing to "Withdrawn after your update", and the argument's weight dropping from 9.0 to 3.6.

**Say:**
> "The AI saw angry messages about his partner Riya and suggested keeping some distance. What it can’t see is that they made up in person. Sabha asks. Aarav tells it, Living Memory records it, and the AI withdraws the suggestion. Relationships are Only You: the AI can inform, but never acts or recommends."

---

## 3:50 to 4:10 · Insights and close

**Do:** click **Insights**.
**Point at:** the Informed Agency Rate, then the metrics table.

**Say:**
> "Sabha measures one thing above all: the Informed Agency Rate. That’s the share of important decisions where the person saw the context, the alternatives and the influences, and chose before anything happened. Showing more information doesn’t count on its own.
>
> AI agents will make more and more decisions for us. Sabha makes sure we still understand them, challenge them, and decide. This is Sabha."

## Recording tips

- **Pause about one second after every click** so the screen settles before you speak about it.
- **Record narration separately if you can.** Screen first, voice over it afterwards, usually sounds calmer.
- **If something goes wrong, keep recording.** Pause, redo that one step, and cut the gap later.
- **Say "concept prototype" once,** or let the footer say it: "Concept prototype. Simulated agent and data."
- **Rename the video file** to `<your name>_<your college>` before submitting.
